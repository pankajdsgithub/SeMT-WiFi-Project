# Payment Schedule
## SeMT Wi‑Fi Project Payment Structure

## 1. Payment Principle
Payments shall be made to the successful bidder against mutually agreed milestones and on the basis of acceptance of deliverables. The schedule should preserve the government’s interest while ensuring vendor cash flow for execution.

## 2. Indicative Payment Milestone Structure
| Milestone | Percentage of Contract Value | Conditions for Payment |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Mobilisation and site survey | 10% | submission of survey report, design baseline, mobilisation plan |
| Supply of equipment | 30% | delivery of approved equipment to project sites |
| Installation and integration | 30% | completion of installation and cabling, configuration of network |
| Testing and commissioning | 20% | completion of testing, acceptance, and sign-off |
| Training and handover | 5% | training completion and final documentation handover |
| Warranty and support / retention | 5% | final support activation and acceptance certificate |

## 3. Performance Security
The bidder may be required to submit:
- earnest money deposit (EMD),
- performance bank guarantee (PBG),
- security deposit as per tender conditions.

## 4. Payment Conditions
- invoices shall be raised against completed stages,
- payment subject to verification by the project authority,
- withheld amounts may be released after correction of defects,
- delays in submission of required documents may delay payment,
- no advance payment unless specifically authorised by the tender conditions.

## 5. Warranty / AMC Stage
The final 5% of value may be released upon successful completion of:
- final acceptance,
- training completion,
- website or dashboard documentation handover,
- maintenance plan activation,
- issue resolution of initial defects.

## 6. Recommendation
The payment schedule should be aligned with deliverable-based acceptance to avoid disputes and maintain accountability across the project lifecycle.
