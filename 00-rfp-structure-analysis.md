# RFP Structure Analysis for SeMT Wi‑Fi Project

## 1. Reference Source
The reference tender reviewed is the OCAC RFP titled:
"Selection of System Integrator (SI) for Campus Wi‑Fi Implementation at State Public Universities under Higher Education Department, Government of Odisha."

The tender reference noted in the document is:
- OCAC-SEGP-INFRA-0029-2026/ENQ/26049
- Dated: 03.08.2026

## 2. Actual Structure Used by OCAC
The document follows a formal government procurement template. The major sections are:

### 2.1 Fact Sheet
This typically contains:
- tender reference,
- title and objective,
- implementing authority,
- university names,
- submission timeline,
- bid submission mode through e-tender portal.

### 2.2 Proposal and Background Information
This section introduces:
- the purpose of the project,
- the role of OCAC,
- the nature of the Wi‑Fi implementation,
- the university network environment and the need for modernization.

### 2.3 Scope of Work
The RFP usually defines the technical scope such as:
- site survey and network assessment,
- network design,
- equipment supply,
- implementation and integration,
- security implementation,
- testing and commissioning,
- documentation and training,
- operations and maintenance,
- service-level commitments.

### 2.4 Instructions to Bidders
This is one of the most important sections and includes:
- general instructions,
- completeness of the RFP,
- acceptance/rejection rights,
- EMD requirements,
- performance bank guarantee,
- pre-bid queries and clarifications,
- submission procedures,
- authentication of bids,
- language, venue, deadline and late bid conditions.

### 2.5 Criteria for Evaluation
The evaluation section normally has:
- pre-qualification criteria,
- technical bid evaluation criteria,
- commercial bid evaluation criteria.

This means the project is not selected only on cost; technical capability and eligibility are weighted before price is considered.

### 2.6 Appointment of Vendor
This section includes:
- award notification,
- issue of purchase order,
- failure to agree with tender terms,
- role matrix,
- project deliverables and time schedule.

### 2.7 General Terms and Conditions of Contract
This section governs:
- buyer details,
- cost of proposal,
- amendments,
- arithmetic correction,
- price validity,
- payment schedule,
- technical information requests,
- acceptance and commissioning,
- operation support,
- performance guarantee,
- liquidated damages,
- termination conditions,
- force majeure,
- dispute resolution,
- warranty and SLA obligations.

### 2.8 Annexures / Bid Forms
The RFP includes annexure forms such as:
- bidder particulars,
- qualification data,
- unit rate / price schedules,
- technical compliance forms,
- declaration forms,
- price bid templates.

## 3. What a SeMT Project RFP Looks Like in Practice
A typical SeMT procurement document is built around a structured lifecycle:

1. Invitation and background
2. Scope and objectives
3. Eligibility and bidder qualification
4. Technical requirements and solution design
5. Draft commercial structure
6. Evaluation model
7. Legal, warranty and SLA conditions
8. Submission and approval process

## 4. Lessons for a New SeMT Wi‑Fi Project
For a similar SeMT Wi‑Fi project, the document should include:
- project background and justifying need,
- list of campus locations,
- scope of services and deliverables,
- technical standards and compliance requirements,
- proposal format and submission rules,
- eligibility and financial capacity checks,
- project schedule and milestone plan,
- payment milestones and performance security,
- service warranty and support obligations,
- risk, governance, and SLA terms.

## 5. Recommended Document Pattern for SeMT Wi‑Fi
Use the following template for a new SeMT Wi‑Fi project:
- Title and reference
- Fact sheet
- Background and rationale
- Scope of work
- Bidder instructions
- Qualification requirements
- Technical requirements
- Evaluation and scoring
- Commercial terms
- Project schedule
- Contract obligations and SLAs
- Annexures

## 6. Conclusion
The OCAC campus Wi‑Fi RFP is a strong example of how government tenders are drafted in Odisha: it combines technical scope, governance, evaluation logic, contract discipline, and procurement compliance in one document. For a SeMT Wi‑Fi project, the same template is appropriate and should be adapted to the specific institutions, locations, and technology requirements of the project.
