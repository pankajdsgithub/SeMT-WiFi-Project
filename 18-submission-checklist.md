# Submission Checklist
## SeMT Wi‑Fi Project Proposal Package

## 1. Mandatory Submission Documents
- Cover page and title page
- Forwarding letter / covering letter
- Tender acknowledgement and proposal form
- Technical proposal
- Commercial proposal
- BOQ and price schedule
- Company profile and legal documents
- Financial statements
- Similar project references
- Technical compliance matrix
- Implementation plan and project schedule
- Risk register and mitigation plan
- Warranty and support SLA
- Training and handover plan
- Approved design summary and drawings
- Bidder declaration forms
- Performance security / EMD documents, if applicable

## 2. Submission Control Points
- all pages signed and stamped,
- all pages serially numbered,
- no missing annexure,
- all required PDFs uploaded and named properly,
- declaration by authorised signatory,
- all forms in the correct format,
- deliverables checked against tender checklist.

## 3. Final Submission Statement
This checklist shall be used as the final validation tool before proposal submission to ensure completeness, consistency and procurement readiness.
