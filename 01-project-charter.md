# Project Charter
## SeMT Wi‑Fi Project for State Public Universities

## 1. Project Title
Campus Wi‑Fi Implementation in Selected State Public Universities under Higher Education Department, Government of Odisha

## 2. Project Objective
To provide seamless, secure, scalable and high-performance wireless connectivity across university campuses, supporting academic learning, administration, research, digital governance and student access requirements.

## 3. Project Sponsorship
- Department: Higher Education Department, Government of Odisha
- Supporting Technical Authority: Odisha Computer Application Centre (OCAC)
- Project Ownership: University administration and department nodal officers

## 4. Programme Context
The project is designed to address the need for robust Wi‑Fi infrastructure in public universities to support:
- classroom connectivity,
- student and faculty mobility,
- administration and digital service delivery,
- visitor access and campus operations,
- centralized management and monitoring.

## 5. Project Scope
The project includes:
- site survey and network assessment,
- network design and architecture,
- supply of networking equipment,
- installation and integration,
- security hardening,
- testing and commissioning,
- training and documentation,
- operations and maintenance support.

## 6. Key Locations
The reference RFP covers the following institutions:
- Utkal University, Bhubaneswar
- Gangadhar Meher University, Sambalpur
- Shree Jagannath Sanskrit Viswavidyalaya, Puri

## 7. Project Outcomes
The expected outcomes are:
- pervasive campus Wi‑Fi coverage,
- secure and role-based user access,
- central monitoring and analytics,
- support for digital learning and administration,
- improved campus digital infrastructure readiness.

## 8. Governance Structure
- Project Sponsor: Higher Education Department / University Authority
- Technical Advisory Body: OCAC / departmental technical team
- Implementing Partner: System Integrator / Technology Vendor
- Project Manager: appointed by the department or execution authority

## 9. Constraints and Assumptions
- Site survey will validate final equipment quantities and placement.
- Existing network assets may require augmentation.
- Coverage may be designed for academic blocks, administrative offices, libraries, hostels, and common areas.
- Security and access policy must be aligned with institutional usage rules.

## 10. Approval Status
This charter is intended as a project baseline and should be validated during the formal tendering, site assessment, and technical design stage.
