# Bill of Quantities (BOQ) and Cost Estimate
## SeMT Wi‑Fi Project for State Public Universities

## 1. Purpose
This BOQ is a planning estimate for the Wi‑Fi implementation project at the selected state public universities. It is intended for technical evaluation, budget planning and tender comparison. Final quantities and rates shall be confirmed during the detailed site survey and technical design validation stage.

## 2. Basis of Estimate
The estimate is framed on a standard university campus deployment model covering:
- academic blocks,
- libraries,
- administrative buildings,
- hostels / residential blocks where required,
- common areas,
- outdoor coverage,
- control room / data center integration,
- central management network.

## 3. Assumed Deployment Scale
The estimate below assumes a medium-size wireless deployment across three universities, with a common campus architecture and site-specific adaptation.

## 6. Cost Heads
The major cost heads are:
- hardware and licensing,
- network infrastructure and cabling,
- installation and integration,
- project management and engineering,
- testing, commissioning and training,
- contingency and operational readiness.

## 7. Exclusions / Clarifications
The following may be treated as extra items if not specifically included in the final scope:
- major civil works beyond standard mounting support,
- heavy structural reinforcement,
- external power augmentation beyond normal connectivity,
- additional project management for major scope changes,
- non-standard extended warranty beyond the agreed period.

## 8. Recommendation
The BOQ should be used as a tender estimate and must be revised after the field survey, room-wise coverage planning and final architecture review. This ensures a realistic and defensible project cost basis.
