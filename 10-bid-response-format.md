# Bid Response Format
## Tender Response Structure for SeMT Wi‑Fi Project

## 1. Bid Submission Structure
Each bidder shall submit the proposal in the following format:

### Part A: Cover Letter
- bidder name,
- address,
- contact details,
- tender reference,
- acknowledgement of RFP conditions,
- declaration of compliance.

### Part B: Eligibility and Legal Documents
- company registration certificate,
- GST registration,
- PAN / tax registration,
- proof of legal status,
- audited financial statements,
- declaration regarding blacklisting or litigation,
- authority to submit bid.

### Part C: Technical Proposal
1. Company profile and experience
2. Similar project references
3. Proposed solution architecture
4. Network design and AP layout approach
5. List of proposed equipment and OEMs
6. Project implementation methodology
7. Risk management approach
8. Testing and acceptance plan
9. Training plan
10. O&M support proposal

### Part D: Commercial Proposal
- BOQ with rates,
- itemized pricing,
- optional items, if any,
- total price,
- payment schedule,
- warranty cost and AMC, if applicable.

### Part E: Supporting Annexures
- technical compliance matrix,
- implementation schedule,
- staff deployment plan,
- certifications and OEM authorizations,
- list of key personnel,
- warranty and SLA declaration.

## 2. Response Format Guidelines
Bidders shall ensure:
- all pages are numbered,
- all documents are signed and stamped,
- technical and commercial bids are submitted as per tender instructions,
- supporting documents are in legible scanned PDF format,
- all forms are completed without omissions,
- any deviations are clearly stated with justification.

## 3. Technical Compliance Matrix Template
| Requirement | Specification | Compliance Status | Supporting Documents |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wi‑Fi standard | 802.11ax support | Yes/No | OEM Datasheet |
| AP type | Indoor/Outdoor enterprise AP | Yes/No | Product catalogue |
| Controller | Centralized management | Yes/No | Design diagram |
| Security | WPA2/WPA3 + segmentation | Yes/No | Security plan |
| Switch | Managed PoE+ | Yes/No | Datasheet |
| SLA | Warranty and support | Yes/No | Service proposal |

## 4. Declaration Format
The bidder shall submit a declaration affirming:
- acceptance of all tender conditions,
- willingness to meet technical and commercial obligations,
- compliance with the implementation schedule,
- no deviation from the agreed scope except as expressly noted.

## 5. Submission Checklist
- signed cover letter,
- eligibility documents,
- technical proposal,
- commercial bid,
- pricing schedule,
- BOQ and rates,
- compliance matrix,
- project references,
- financial statements,
- EMD if applicable.

## 6. Recommendation
The response format should remain clear, structured and auditable. For government tenders, the documentation quality often affects the technical evaluation outcome as much as the price itself.
