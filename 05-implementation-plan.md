# Implementation Plan

## 1. Stage-wise Execution
The project should be implemented in phases to reduce risk and allow controlled handover.

### Stage 1: Project Mobilisation
- project kickoff,
- mobilisation of team,
- review of campus and site conditions,
- finalisation of design assumptions.

### Stage 2: Site Survey and Design Finalization
- building-wise survey,
- AP placement decision,
- cable path planning,
- equipment list finalisation,
- review with university and OCAC representatives.

### Stage 3: Supply and Logistics
- procurement of equipment,
- delivery scheduling,
- warehouse and site staging,
- verification of item quality and accessories.

### Stage 4: Installation and Integration
- installation of APs,
- mounting and cabling,
- switch integration,
- controller and management setup,
- network segmentation and security policies.

### Stage 5: Testing and Optimization
- signal coverage check,
- user connectivity and roaming tests,
- SSA / throughput validation,
- authentication and guest network testing,
- optimization of channel and power settings.

### Stage 6: Training and Handover
- operator and admin training,
- submission of as-built documents,
- final acceptance testing,
- support transition and warranty registration.

## 2. Delivery Milestones
The government tender usually expects milestones aligned with realistic implementation timelines and controlled procurement workflow. These can be adapted to the specific university campus schedule and site readiness.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria
The system shall be accepted only when:
- all required locations are covered,
- signal strength meets agreed standards,
- access policy works as expected,
- central management is functional,
- performance and security checks are complete,
- training and documentation are delivered.
