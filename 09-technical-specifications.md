# Technical Specifications
## Campus Wi‑Fi System for State Public Universities

## 1. Purpose
This technical specification defines the minimum technical standards expected from the System Integrator for the design, supply, installation and commissioning of a campus Wi‑Fi network in the selected universities.

## 2. Standard Requirements
The Wi‑Fi solution shall be based on enterprise-class, standards-compliant, scalable wireless technology capable of supporting large numbers of users and application workloads in academic environments.

## 3. Wi‑Fi Network Standards
The system shall support:
- IEEE 802.11a/b/g/n/ac/ax standards, as applicable,
- dual-band or tri-band operation,
- secure SSID segmentation,
- seamless roaming and fast handoff across APs,
- high-density campus operation,
- support for guest and institutional traffic segmentation.

## 4. Access Point Requirements
Access points shall be enterprise-grade and shall provide:
- support for indoor and outdoor deployment,
- PoE+ or equivalent powered operation,
- centralized management through controller or cloud platform,
- secure management over encrypted channels,
- MU-MIMO support where applicable,
- coverage planning for classroom, corridor, office and open-area coverage,
- support for WPA2/WPA3 and 802.1X or equivalent secure authentication.

## 5. Controller / Management Requirements
The management platform shall provide:
- centralized device management,
- AP provisioning and health reporting,
- RF optimization and channel planning,
- client monitoring and event logs,
- policy-based access control,
- guest access control and band steering features,
- analytics and alarm conditions for AP health.

## 6. Network Switching Requirements
The network shall include:
- managed PoE+ switches,
- VLAN support,
- QoS support for voice and video traffic,
- link redundancy or stacking features where required,
- support for monitoring and remote management,
- uplink capacity aligned to AP density and user load.

## 7. Security Requirements
The solution shall include:
- role-based access control,
- separate SSIDs for staff, students, guest, and management access,
- secure authentication and encryption,
- firewall or segmentation policies,
- centralized logging and access control review,
- device registration or policy validation where required.

## 8. Outdoor and High-Density Coverage Requirements
Outdoor access points shall be suitable for:
- vehicle movement areas,
- boundaries and perimeters,
- open courtyards,
- visitor and event zones,
- weather-resistant operation.

For high-density areas such as classrooms, libraries and common halls, the design shall ensure:
- adequate AP density,
- minimal co-channel interference,
- effective load balancing,
- strong coverage and user experience.

## 9. Cabling and Connectivity Requirements
The contractor shall provide:
- structured cabling for AP connectivity,
- fibre backbone for long stretches and building interconnection,
- rack and patch infrastructure,
- proper cable management,
- termination and testing documentation,
- support for future growth.

## 10. Power and Backup Requirements
The design shall include:
- PoE-based operation wherever feasible,
- UPS support for critical network devices,
- power backup for core switches and controllers,
- proper electrical coordination with the university facilities team.

## 11. Testing and Acceptance Requirements
The system shall be tested for:
- signal strength and coverage,
- AP uptime and health,
- roaming and handoff continuity,
- user authentication and access validation,
- throughput under realistic load,
- guest and student access segmentation,
- backup and failover behavior.

## 12. Documentation Requirements
The vendor shall provide:
- final as-built drawings,
- installation records,
- IP and logical design documentation,
- AP placement maps,
- switch and rack layout,
- commissioning report,
- user manuals and admin guides,
- training material.

## 13. Operations and Maintenance Requirements
The contractor shall provide:
- warranty support,
- preventive maintenance,
- troubleshooting and replacement support,
- spare part management,
- SLA-based service support,
- monthly or periodic health review reports.

## 14. Compliance Requirements
The solution shall conform to relevant standards and good practices, including but not limited to:
- enterprise Wi‑Fi requirements,
- secure wireless network policy,
- interoperability standards,
- relevant local and national regulatory obligations.

## 15. Conclusion
These specifications define the minimum expected technology and service standard for the SeMT Wi‑Fi project. Final metrics and quantities shall be validated through formal site survey and engineering review before commercial finalisation.
