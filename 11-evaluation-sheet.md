## Bid Evaluation Matrix for SeMT Wi‑Fi Project

## 1. Evaluation Method
The bid shall be evaluated in three stages:
1. Eligibility and pre-qualification screening
2. Technical bid evaluation
3. Commercial bid evaluation

## 5. Final Ranking Formula
A simple model may be used as:
- Technical Score = 70% contribution
- Commercial Score = 30% contribution

The technically qualified lowest responsive bidder may be selected subject to compliance with the tender conditions.

## 6. Evaluation Notes
- non-compliant bids should be rejected,
- bids with major deviations should be assessed with caution,
- incomplete response packages should be considered non-responsive,
- price cannot override technical non-compliance.

## 7. Recommendation
The evaluation should remain objective, transparent and consistent with government procurement practices. Technical and service reliability should carry greater weight than a small difference in commercial price.
